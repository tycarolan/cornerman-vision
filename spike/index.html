Idle. Tap Start and grant camera access. Prop the phone at ~45° to your stance, full body in frame.

What to look for. Sharp isolated spikes crossing the threshold = detectable. A rounded smear, or guard jitter reaching the same height as a punch, = the signal isn't separable and the product narrows to work rate + guard only. Frames/punch under 4 means classification is dead on arrival.

Work in blocks. Declare the punch type, context and rep count before throwing, then Start Block, throw exactly that many, and End Block. Detected-against-expected is the recall number the model decision turns on, and it cannot be recovered afterwards from an unlabelled trace. Blocks accumulate across model switches; one Export carries the whole session. Reload the page to start a clean session — nothing else clears it.
